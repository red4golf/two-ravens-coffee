Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

What We Collect

We keep this simple. The only information we collect directly is what you give us: your name, email address, and message through the contact form, or your email address if you ask to be notified when a roast goes live.

Orders and Payments

Purchases are processed by Stripe. Your payment details go directly to Stripe and never touch this website. Stripe shares your name, shipping address, and order details with us so we can fulfill your order, and we pass that information to our roasting partner, Roastify, solely to roast and ship your coffee.

How We Use Your Information

To respond to your messages, fulfill your orders, and — only if you asked — let you know when a roast launches. That's it. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with anyone for marketing purposes.

Services We Rely On

Behind the scenes, this site uses a small set of services that handle data on our behalf: Stripe (payments), Roastify (fulfillment and shipping), Make.com and Slack (routing contact-form messages to us), and GitHub Pages (website hosting). Each processes only what it needs to do its job.

Cookies and Tracking

This site does not run analytics or advertising trackers. Embedded services (such as Stripe checkout) may set their own cookies as part of doing their job.

Email

If you joined a notify list, every email we send includes a way to opt out, and you can email us any time to be removed.

Your Choices

Want to know what we have, or want it deleted? Send a note through the contact form and we'll take care of it promptly.

Children

This site sells coffee and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.

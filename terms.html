Terms of Sale

Last updated: July 2026

Who We Are

Two Ravens Coffee Co. is a small-batch coffee brand based in Washington State, USA. Orders are roasted to order and fulfilled by our roasting partner, Roastify. Questions reach us fastest through the contact form.

Orders

Placing an order is an offer to purchase. We accept your order when it ships. If a pricing or listing error slips through, we may cancel the affected order and refund you in full before it ships — we'll tell you why.

Pricing, Shipping, and Promotions

Prices are in US dollars. Shipping is charged as described in our Shipping Policy. Promotional offers (such as free shipping on orders of two or more bags through August 1, 2026) apply only during their stated window and may be modified or withdrawn for future orders at any time.

Payment

Payments are processed by Stripe. Your card is charged at checkout. We ship to United States addresses only.

Fulfillment

Because every bag is roasted after you order it, most orders ship within 4 to 5 business days. See the Shipping Policy for details and the Return & Refund Policy for cancellations, damage, and refunds.

The Give-Back

A portion of sales from our mission roasts supports Angel Wings Transport, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. Your purchase is a retail purchase, not a charitable donation, and is not tax-deductible. Coffee purchases are not compensation for air transportation; all Angel Wings Transport flights are conducted by volunteer pilots under FAA Part 91.

Limitation of Liability

To the fullest extent permitted by law, our liability for any claim arising from an order is limited to the amount you paid for that order.

Governing Law

Changes

We may update these terms from time to time; the version posted at the time of your order applies to that order.
